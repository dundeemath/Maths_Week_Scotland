Why leaves crinkle?

Why are some leaves more wrinkled than others? And what causes this to happen?

Consider a circular disc of radius, \(R\).

The perimeter is \(2\pi R\) whilst the area of the circle is \(\pi R^2\). It can be shown that this circle is the most efficient way to contain the area \(\pi R^2\).

Now imagine distorting the disc so that the circumference is wrinkled. The circumference of the wrinkled shape is larger than \(2 \pi R\).

In the plant at this stop, notice that the leaves are wrinkled rather than smooth. Why does evolution find this shape for this particular plant and how does it happen?

One way to make a wrinkled surface is to have different regions of the surface grow at different rates. Essentially, take a circle and allow the outer region to grow at a faster rate than the inner region. The effect of the differential growth is that the faster growing region wrinkles.
